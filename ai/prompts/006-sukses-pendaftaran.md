# 006 — sukses-pendaftaran

## Purpose

Celebrate a finished registration and hint at the registration card.

## Composition

- Square, the two characters centred from the knees up.
- Confetti around, not covering faces.

## Characters (see cast in `ai/style-guide.md`)

- Aisyah (white hijab, school uniform) holding up a card with both hands, proud smile.
- Ibu Sari beside her, hands clasped, happy.

## Setting and props

- The card shows an abstract square pattern like a QR code (not readable) and two grey lines.
- Small confetti shapes in sand, sage and a few blue pieces.

## Colour and light

Cream and sage; blue in a few confetti pieces and the card corner.

## Avoid

- A readable or real QR code
- Numbers on the card
- Trophies or medals
- Everything listed under "Never" and "Rules for every image" in the style guide
