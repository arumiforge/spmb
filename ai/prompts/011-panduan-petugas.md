# 011 — panduan-petugas

## Purpose

Learning the system together, relaxed and helpful.

## Composition

- Wide landscape; subjects in the RIGHT two thirds.
- LEFT third almost empty: soft cream wash fading to white.

## Characters (see cast in `ai/style-guide.md`)

- Bu Nur pointing at an open booklet, explaining.
- Pak Budi (glasses, batik-like shirt) nodding with a smile, hand on the laptop.

## Setting and props

- A shared wooden desk with a laptop (abstract screen), an open booklet with grey lines and simple pictures, two cups of tea.
- A window with soft light and a plant behind them.

## Colour and light

Cream, sand, sage; blue only on the booklet cover and a small laptop detail.

## Avoid

- Readable text in the booklet
- Letters in batik patterns
- An office with many people
- Everything listed under "Never" and "Rules for every image" in the style guide
