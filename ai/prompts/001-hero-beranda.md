# 001 — hero-beranda

## Purpose

First thing parents see: a warm welcome that says "registering is easy and friendly".

## Composition

- Wide landscape. All subjects in the RIGHT 55%.
- LEFT 45% stays almost empty: a very soft cream wash fading into white, no objects, no people (headline goes there).
- Eye level, characters standing on a gentle path, full bodies visible.

## Characters (see cast in `ai/style-guide.md`)

- Ibu Sari (mother, white hijab, sand tunic) with a hand on Aisyah's shoulder, both smiling.
- Aisyah (12, white hijab, white shirt, navy skirt, sage backpack) holding a smartphone; the screen shows only a few abstract blue and white blocks.
- In the background, small and soft: Raka and another student walking towards the school, waving.

## Setting and props

- A simple single-storey Indonesian school building behind them: cream walls, muted terracotta roof tiles, a covered walkway, no sign or name.
- Two rounded trees in sage green, a few potted plants, a low fence.
- Sky: pale cream with one or two soft clouds.

## Colour and light

Mostly cream, sand and sage; the only blue accent is the phone screen.

## Avoid

- Any sign, banner or name on the building
- Flags
- A crowded scene
- Realistic faces
- Everything listed under "Never" and "Rules for every image" in the style guide
