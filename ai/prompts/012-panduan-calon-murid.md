# 012 — panduan-calon-murid

## Purpose

A family preparing for registration together at home, at ease.

## Composition

- Wide landscape; family in the RIGHT two thirds.
- LEFT third almost empty: soft cream wash fading to white.

## Characters (see cast in `ai/style-guide.md`)

- Ibu Sari and Aisyah sitting close on a sofa looking at a smartphone.
- Pak Hadi standing behind the sofa, leaning in, smiling.

## Setting and props

- A simple sofa in sage with a cushion, a small side table with a plant, a framed abstract painting on the wall (shapes only).
- Phone screen shows abstract blocks.

## Colour and light

Sage sofa, cream walls, sand floor; blue only on the cushion or phone case.

## Avoid

- Readable phone screen
- Family photos with faces on the wall
- A busy room
- Everything listed under "Never" and "Rules for every image" in the style guide
