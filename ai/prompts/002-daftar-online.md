# 002 — daftar-online

## Purpose

Show that registering online is something a parent and child do together at home.

## Composition

- Tall portrait. Characters and table in the LOWER two thirds.
- Upper third: three or four floating abstract items.
- Isolated vignette, soft painted edges, transparent around it.

## Characters (see cast in `ai/style-guide.md`)

- Pak Hadi (father, abstract batik-like shirt) sitting at a small wooden table, typing on a laptop.
- Raka (12, white shirt, navy trousers) standing beside him, pointing at the laptop screen, excited.

## Setting and props

- Small wooden table, a cup of tea, a small plant.
- Laptop screen shows only abstract blocks.
- Floating above: two rounded form cards with grey lines, a document with a blue check mark, an upload arrow inside a small blue circle.

## Colour and light

Warm sand and cream; blue only on the check mark and the upload circle.

## Avoid

- Readable text on the screen or cards
- A full room background
- Paper texture outside the vignette
- Everything listed under "Never" and "Rules for every image" in the style guide

## Prompt

```
Hand-painted storybook illustration in soft gouache and watercolour, visible gentle brush texture,
slightly uneven painted edges, light paper grain inside the painted areas. Stylised friendly characters
with simple rounded faces, small dot eyes, rosy cheeks and simplified hands, like a modern Indonesian
children's educational book. Not photorealistic, not semi-realistic, not 3D, not CGI, no glossy shading,
no anime. Warm muted palette: cream, warm sand, slate grey, muted sage green and soft terracotta, with ONE
small accent in medium blue #2563EB. Soft even morning light. Absolutely no text, letters, numbers,
logos, signatures or watermarks anywhere.

Portrait (tall) image on a real transparent background; the painting is an isolated vignette with soft painted edges. In the lower two thirds: a father with short hair and a simple shirt with an abstract sage and sand batik-like pattern (no letters) sits at a small wooden table typing on a laptop, with a cup of tea and a small potted plant beside it. His 12-year-old son, short black hair, white school shirt and navy trousers, stands next to him and points at the laptop screen with an excited smile. The screen shows only abstract light blocks. In the upper third, floating softly: two rounded form cards with grey lines instead of text, a small document with a blue check mark, and an upload arrow inside a small blue circle.
```

## Alt text (Indonesian, used in the view)

Orang tua dan anak mengisi formulir pendaftaran online bersama.
