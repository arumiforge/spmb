# 004 — kosong-data

## Purpose

Gentle "nothing here yet" without looking like an error.

## Composition

- Square, subject centred, about 60% of the width; lots of empty space around.
- No people.

## Characters (see cast in `ai/style-guide.md`)

- None.

## Setting and props

- An open, empty folder in warm sand.
- Three soft blank paper sheets floating out of it at slight angles.
- A small potted plant in a terracotta pot beside it.

## Colour and light

Sand, cream, sage; a small blue tab on the folder is the only accent.

## Avoid

- Writing on the papers
- Dust, cobwebs or anything sad
- Too many details (it is shown small)
- Everything listed under "Never" and "Rules for every image" in the style guide
