## What you can do here

- Read the new student admission information of {nama_sekolah}.
- Register online from a phone or computer, without creating an account.
- Upload the required documents and print the registration card.
- Check the registration status and the test schedule.

> **Note:** Online registration is not open yet. The full steps will be added to this page when registration opens.

## Before you register

Prepare the following documents as clear photos or scans:

1. The family card (Kartu Keluarga, KK).
2. The future student's birth certificate.
3. The school report or a letter from the previous school.
4. A passport photo of the future student with a plain background.

> **Tip:** Photograph documents in a bright place, lay them flat on a table, and make sure all the writing can be read. Files that are too large are made smaller automatically.

## If something goes wrong

- If a page does not open, check the internet connection and reload the page.
- If you forget the registration number, contact the admission team and give the future student's full name and date of birth.
