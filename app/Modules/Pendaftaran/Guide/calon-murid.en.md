## Registering online

The form is filled in step by step. At the top there are step numbers joined by a line. Finished steps get a tick. On a phone it says, for example, "Step 2 of 5".

1. Open the [Register](/daftar) page on a phone or computer.
2. **Step 1, Future student:** fill in the name, sex, place and date of birth and the other details. Press **Next**.
3. **Step 2, Address:** write the street and RT/RW, then choose the village from the list (see [Filling in the address](#filling-in-the-address)). Press **Next**.
4. **Step 3, Parents or guardian:** fill in the father's and mother's details. Then answer the question about a guardian: choose **No guardian** if the future student lives with the birth father or mother, or **Has a guardian** if someone else looks after them, for example a grandparent or an uncle. The guardian boxes appear only after you choose **Has a guardian**.
5. **Step 4, Previous school:** type the name of the school and choose it from the list. Press **Next**.
6. **Step 5, Check and send:** read the summary of all the details. Press **Change** next to a part that is wrong to correct it. When everything is right, tick the agreement statement and press **Send registration**.
7. Write down or take a photo of the **registration number** that appears, and keep the **private link**, for example press **Save to WhatsApp** and send it to your own number.

> **Tip:** Boxes without the word "optional" must be filled in. What you have typed is not lost when you press **Back** or the browser's back button. The name and date of birth must match the birth certificate.

- If **Next** does not move to the next step, a box has not been filled in. That box has red writing under it.
- If a red dot appears on one of the step numbers after sending, an answer in that step needs fixing. The form opens that step for you. Correct the answer and send again.
- If the Register page says "Online registration is closed for now", no round is open yet or the places are all taken. See the round schedule or contact the admission team.
- If you lose the private link, open your details through [Check status](/cek-status) with the registration number and the date of birth.

## Filling in the address

The village (desa or kelurahan), district (kecamatan), regency or city, and province are chosen from the official list, so you do not have to type them all.

1. In the **Address (street, RT/RW)** box, write the street, house number and RT/RW.
2. In the **Search for your village** box, type the name of your village, for example *Ploso*. To find it faster, add the name of the district, for example *Ploso Jati*.
3. Choose the right village from the list. Under each village you see its district, regency or city, and province.
4. The province, regency or city, district and village are filled in for you. Check them once more.

You can also choose them one by one: first the **Province**, then the **Regency or city**, the **District** and the **Village**. Each list appears after the one before it is chosen.

> **Tip:** Use the address written on the family card (Kartu Keluarga). The school's province and regency are often already chosen. Change them if you live somewhere else.

- If your village is not in the list, press **My place is not in the list, I will type it** and write the village, district, regency or city, and province yourself. The admission team will match it later.
- If the list does not appear or it says "The list could not be loaded", check your internet connection. You can still type the address yourself.

## Checking the registration status

1. Open the [Check status](/cek-status) page.
2. Type the **registration number** and choose the future student's **date of birth**.
3. Press **Check status**.
4. The **Applicant page** shows the registration status, the documents status and the details you entered.
5. When you are done, press **Sign out**, especially on someone else's phone or computer.

- If it says "No record was found", check the registration number and the date of birth again.
- If it says "Too many attempts", wait one minute and try again.
- If you forget the registration number, contact the admission team and give the future student's full name and date of birth.

## Correcting the address and contact details

1. Open the **Applicant page** through Check status or the private link.
2. Press **Correct the address and contact details**.
3. Change the address, mobile number, WhatsApp number or email. To move to another village, search for the new village or choose again starting with the province.
4. Press **Save changes**.

> **Note:** The name, date of birth, NIK, NISN and other identity details can only be changed by the admission team. Contact them if something is wrong.
