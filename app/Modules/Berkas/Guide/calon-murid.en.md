## Uploading the registration documents

Documents are sent from your registration status page. Photos from a phone camera can be uploaded directly; they are made smaller automatically.

1. Open **Check status** and enter the registration number and the future student's date of birth (or open the private link you received after registering).
2. Scroll down to **Registration documents**.
3. Next to the document you want to send, press **Choose file**. On a phone you can take a photo straight away or choose one from the gallery.
4. Press **Upload**. Wait until a message says the document has been sent.
5. Repeat for the other documents.

> **Tip:** Photograph documents in a bright place, lay them flat on a table and make sure all the writing can be read. Do not cut off any part.

## Downloading an example document

For some documents the school provides an example or a letter template, for example the letter confirming the child is still a pupil, which the head of the primary school (SD/MI) fills in and signs.

1. Look for the **Download the example** link under the name of the document, on the **Requirements** page or in **Registration documents**.
2. Press the link. The example file is saved on your phone or computer.
3. Print the example and take it to the previous primary school (SD/MI) to be filled in, stamped and signed.
4. Photograph or scan the finished letter and upload it like the other documents.

> **Note:** The link only appears when the school provides an example. Anyone can download it, without a registration number.

## Documents sent later

Some documents, for example the letter confirming the child is still a pupil, the school report or the graduation certificate (SKL), may be sent later.

1. Next to a document you do not have yet, press **Send it later**.
2. When you have it, come back to the status page and upload it as usual.

> **Note:** If the admission team sets a deadline, the date is shown in **Registration documents**.

## What the document statuses mean

- **Waiting to be checked**: the document has been sent and the admission team will check it.
- **Accepted**: the document has been checked and accepted.
- **Needs replacing**: read the admission team's note, then press **Upload again** and send the right document.
- **To follow**: you marked this document to be sent later.

## If something goes wrong

- If the message "The file is too large" appears, make the photo or scan smaller and try again.
- If the message "This type of file is not accepted" appears, use a photo (JPG or PNG) or a PDF as shown under the name of the document.
- If a photo from an iPhone cannot be read, open **Settings > Camera > Formats**, choose **Most Compatible**, then take the photo again.
- If a message says your access time has run out, open **Check status** again and enter the registration number and the date of birth.
