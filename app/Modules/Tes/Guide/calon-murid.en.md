## Seeing the test schedule

The test schedule appears on the registration status page once the admission team has shared it.

1. Open **Check status** and enter the registration number and the future student's date of birth (or open your private link).
2. Look at **Test schedule**: the day and date, time, room and location.
3. Read **What to bring** underneath.
4. The same schedule is also printed on the registration card.

> **Tip:** Come early and bring the registration card, printed or on your phone.

- If it says the schedule will be announced, the admission team has not shared the schedule yet. Please look again later.
- If the future student cannot come at that time, contact the admission team. They can move the test to another session.
