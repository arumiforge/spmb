## Downloading the registration card

Bring the registration card to the test and to re-registration.

1. Open the **Check status** page and enter the registration number and the date of birth. You can also open the private link you kept after registering.
2. In **Registration card**, press **Download the card**.
3. Print the card on A4 paper, then cut along the dotted line.
4. Stick a 3 x 4 passport photo in the box provided.

> **Tip:** Keep the card file on your phone too. You can download and print the card again at any time.

- If it says "The card can be downloaded after the admission team has checked the documents", wait until the team has finished checking your details, then look again.
- If you lose the card, download and print it again. If someone else uses the card, contact the admission team to have a new card made.
